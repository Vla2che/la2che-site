Software

AYMON

Seller management for Amazon marketplaces.

Private testing. Not yet open to new sellers.

AYMON is a web application that helps a seller run a store on Amazon from one place. The seller asks questions in plain language and AYMON answers from the seller's own account data.

What AYMON does

  • Watches account health and sends an alert when Amazon reports a problem.
  • Tracks inventory and warns when a product is running low or out of stock.
  • Reviews advertising results and recommends changes to campaigns and keywords.
  • Checks listing text against marketplace content rules before it is published.
  • Drafts replies to routine customer questions for the seller to review.

How it connects

  • AYMON connects through Amazon's official Selling Partner API and Amazon Ads API.
  • A seller authorizes the connection and can withdraw that authorization at any time.
  • AYMON proposes a change, the seller confirms it, and only then is it carried out.

To ask about AYMON, email support@la2che.com.

Software

Tenderloop

Public sector tender alerts for small contractors in the United Kingdom.

In development

Tenderloop is a subscription service that shows a contractor only the public sector contracts they can realistically bid for, matched to their trade, their region and the size of work they take on, with a plain-English summary of what each notice asks for.

It reads the United Kingdom's open public procurement feeds, Contracts Finder and Find a Tender. The first release covers electrical and building services work in North West England.

Tenderloop is not yet open to subscribers.

Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026. This policy covers this website and the AYMON application, both operated by La2che Enterprises LLC.

This website

This website has no forms, accounts or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may record standard technical details of a visit, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

What AYMON collects

  • Seller account details. The name, email address and sign-in details of the seller who uses AYMON.
  • Amazon selling account data. With the seller's authorization, AYMON reads data from that seller's own account through Amazon's Selling Partner API and Amazon Ads API. This includes listings, inventory, orders, sales and fees, account health, and advertising campaigns and results.
  • Customer messages. If a seller routes customer messages to AYMON, those messages are stored so AYMON can sort them and draft replies. They can include a customer's name and what the customer wrote.
  • Activity records. A log of what AYMON recommended, what the seller approved, and what was changed.

How the information is used

  • To provide AYMON to the seller: answers, alerts, reports, recommendations and drafts.
  • To carry out a change on the seller's account after the seller approves it.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We do not sell personal information or Amazon account data. We do not use customer information for marketing. We do not share one seller's data with another seller.

Service providers

AYMON relies on outside companies for hosting and data storage, AI processing and email delivery. They receive only the information needed to perform that work for us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Security

Connections to AYMON are encrypted in transit. Amazon access credentials are stored in encrypted form. Access to AYMON requires sign-in and is limited to the seller and to the people who operate the service.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep information only as long as it is needed to provide the service or to meet legal obligations. When a seller stops using AYMON, the seller can ask us to delete their data by emailing us.

Your choices

A seller can withdraw AYMON's access at any time in Amazon Seller Central, under Apps and Services, Manage Your Apps. Anyone can ask what information we hold about them, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us.

Children

This website and AYMON are intended for businesses and are not directed at children.
